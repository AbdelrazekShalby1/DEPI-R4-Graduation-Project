Description

Epilepsy is a neurological disorder characterized by recurrent seizures caused by abnormal electrical activity in the brain.

Common Symptoms

  • Temporary confusion
  • Staring spells
  • Uncontrolled jerking movements
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Anxiety before seizures

Risk Factors

  • Head injury
  • Stroke
  • Brain infections
  • Genetic factors
  • Brain tumors
