Description

Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) is a condition characterized by gradual loss of kidney function over time. Early detection and treatment can slow disease progression.

Common Symptoms

  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Swelling in ankles and feet
  • Frequent urination
  • Blood in urine
  • Foamy urine

Risk Factors

  • Diabetes
  • High blood pressure
  • Heart disease
  • Family history
  • Age over 60
