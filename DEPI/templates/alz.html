Description

Alzheimer's disease is a progressive neurological disorder that gradually destroys memory, thinking skills, and the ability to perform daily tasks.

Common Symptoms

  • Memory loss
  • Difficulty planning
  • Confusion with time
  • Trouble understanding visual images
  • Problems with speaking

Risk Factors

  • Age over 65
  • Family history
  • Down syndrome
  • Mild cognitive impairment
  • Head trauma
